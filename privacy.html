privacy policy

effective date: april 15, 2026

checksync ("we," "our," or "us") provides a club and school attendance management platform for student organizations, faculty advisors, and students. this policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected.

1. information we collect

  • account information: full name, school or advisor email address, role (advisor or student), and club name. phone numbers are not collected.
  • roster & attendance records: member names, email addresses, optional role or grade tags, check-in timestamps, qr session logs, and attendance status (present, excused, unexcused).
  • absence excuse requests: meeting dates, absence categories, brief explanations, and advisor review notes.
  • schedule & portal announcements: club calendar events and announcements delivered exclusively inside the student portal.

2. how we use information

  • authenticate advisor and student accounts with role separation.
  • record live qr check-ins, roll call attendance, and semester analytics.
  • deliver meeting schedules, automated portal reminders, and advisor announcements inside the student portal.
  • process student absence excuse submissions and advisor approvals.

3. ferpa & student data privacy

checksync is designed for educational clubs and school organizations. student attendance records are accessible only to the authorized club advisor and the individual student (for their own check-ins, excuses, and linked clubs). we do not sell, rent, or monetize student or advisor data, and we display zero third-party advertising.

4. data storage, retention & deletion

data is stored securely on google firebase (authentication and cloud firestore) using encrypted connections. advisors may permanently erase all club roster members and saved attendance logs at any time using the ferpa data reset control in settings, or request account deletion.

5. third-party services

checksync relies on google firebase for authentication and database hosting. when an advisor chooses to follow up with unexcused missing members, gmail compose opens locally in the advisor's browser with bcc recipients pre-filled; checksync does not send automated external emails or sms messages.

6. contact

for privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact your club advisor or reach the checksync team through your school administrator.
